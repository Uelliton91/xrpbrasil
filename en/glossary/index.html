Glossary

An expanding glossary with basic terms from the world of XRP and the XRP Ledger (XRPL). Use it as a quick reference and follow related links when you want deeper context.

  • XRPL: short for XRP Ledger, the public payments network where XRP is the native asset.
  • XRP: digital asset used for fees, base reserves, and bridge liquidity in payments. It is not a company.
  • Ledger: the state of the network, including balances, offers, trustlines, and more. On XRPL, a new ledger typically closes every 3 to 5 seconds.
  • Account: an entity identified by an address beginning with r..., controlled by one or more cryptographic keys.
  • Private key: the secret that allows funds to move from an account. It must remain private at all times.
  • Seed phrase: a word-based representation of the private key. Whoever controls the seed controls the account.
  • UNL: Unique Node List, the set of validators a server trusts for consensus participation.
  • Validator: a server that proposes and votes on transaction sets for each new ledger.
  • Trustline: the trust relationship used for issued assets and IOUs, including limits and rippling settings.
  • IOU: an issued asset on XRPL representing something off-chain, such as USD, EUR, or a stablecoin.
  • Issued currency: another name for an IOU, emphasizing that the asset is issued by a specific account.
  • Stablecoin: an asset designed to track a reference currency, often issued as an IOU or token.
  • RWA: Real World Asset, a real-world asset such as debt, real estate, or commodities represented on a ledger.
  • AMM: Automated Market Maker, an on-ledger liquidity pool that enables automatic swaps between assets.
  • DEX: the native decentralized exchange of XRPL, based on order books for asset pairs.
  • Escrow: temporary locking of XRP under time-based or key-based conditions.
  • Pathfinding: the routing mechanism that searches for the best liquidity path between two points.
  • Rippling: a feature that allows IOU balances to flow across connected accounts; it can be disabled when necessary.
  • Base reserve: the amount of XRP that needs to stay locked in an account so it can exist on the network.
  • Sequence: the transaction counter of an account. Each transaction consumes the next sequence number.
  • Fee: the XRP amount paid on each transaction. It is burned in a very small fraction.
  • Testnet: a testing network that replicates XRPL behavior but uses assets with no financial value.
  • Faucet: a service that distributes test XRP on development networks such as testnet or devnet.
  • Wallet: software or hardware used to manage keys and interact with XRPL.
