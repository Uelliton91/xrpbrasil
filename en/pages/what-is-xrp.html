What is XRP?

XRP is the native digital asset of the XRP Ledger (XRPL). It was designed to make value transfer faster and cheaper, especially in cross-border payment flows.

This page is educational and intentionally concise. For a broader study path, continue with the beginner's guide.

What XRP is used for

  • Transaction fees: every XRPL operation pays a tiny fee in XRP, and that amount is burned.
  • Bridge asset: XRP can help route value between different currencies and issued assets.
  • Base reserve: every account needs to hold a small XRP balance to exist on the ledger.

How XRP works inside XRPL

XRP operates inside a public ledger. XRPL closes new ledgers every few seconds and confirms transactions through deterministic consensus.

Basic risks and safety habits

  • Avoid profit promises, fake airdrops, and impersonation scams.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
  • Use the testnet first if you are still learning how the network behaves.

FAQ

Common questions about XRP

Short answers for new readers.

Is XRP a company?

No. XRP is a digital asset. Ripple is a separate company that builds products around payments and custody.

Do I need to buy XRP to learn?

No. You can use the testnet and educational materials before taking any financial risk.

Is XRP the same thing as XRPL?

No. XRP is the asset. XRPL is the network where that asset is used.
