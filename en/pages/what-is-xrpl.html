What is XRPL (XRP Ledger)?

The XRP Ledger (XRPL) is a public payments network built for fast settlement and very low fees. It works as a global ledger for moving value and issuing digital assets.

If you want the full technical breakdown, continue with XRPL explained.

How XRPL works

  • It closes new ledgers every few seconds.
  • It relies on consensus between independent validators.
  • It does not depend on mining to confirm transactions.

Native features

  • A built-in DEX for exchanging assets.
  • A native AMM with on-ledger liquidity pools.
  • Issued assets, IOUs, and stablecoin support.

What it is used for in practice

XRPL is used in cross-border payments, asset tokenization, and low-cost settlement workflows. XRP is the native asset of that network.

FAQ

Common questions about XRPL

Quick answers about the XRP Ledger.

Is XRPL a blockchain?

It is a distributed ledger with its own consensus mechanism. In practice, it behaves like a public blockchain-style network.

What is the difference between XRPL and Ripple?

XRPL is the public network. Ripple is a company that contributes products and infrastructure to the ecosystem.

How expensive are transactions?

Fees are usually very low and paid in XRP, often at fractions of a cent.
