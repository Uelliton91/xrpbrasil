About the project

XRP BRASIL is an independent educational portal built for the Brazilian community interested in XRP and the XRP Ledger. The English layer exists to make the same infrastructure-first perspective accessible to international readers who follow the project through social channels.

Mission

Explain clearly how the payments ecosystem around XRP and XRPL works, using accessible language, technical rigor, and official references whenever possible.

  • Translate technical concepts such as consensus, DEX, AMM, stablecoins, and real-world assets into practical language.
  • Organize scattered information into guides, articles, glossaries, and tools that are easier to navigate.
  • Keep a critical perspective by separating infrastructure reality from hype and unrealistic promises.

Editorial principles

  • Education first: the project is designed to help readers understand technology and market structure, not to push speculative narratives.
  • Independence: XRP BRASIL is not aligned with price targets, token campaigns, or promotional pushes.
  • Verifiable sourcing: whenever possible, articles point readers to official documentation, formal announcements, or on-chain data.

Transparency

  • The content is strictly educational. We do not offer investment advice.
  • Affiliate links and sponsorships, when present, should be clearly identified.
  • We accept voluntary XRP donations to support the project, but they are optional and carry no financial return or private benefit.
  • We never ask for mandatory deposits, guaranteed returns, seed phrases, private keys, or 2FA codes.

Who runs XRP BRASIL?

The project is maintained by XRPL enthusiasts with experience in technology and communication. The focus is not on personality or branding. The value should come from the usefulness, coherence, and auditability of the content itself.

Contact

For editorial suggestions, corrections, or educational partnerships, write to contato@xrpbrasil.com.br. We do not provide one-on-one support for investments or personal financial decisions.
