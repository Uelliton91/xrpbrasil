Understanding the metrics

Transaction cost

The minimum XRPL fee is 10 drops, where 1 million drops equal 1 XRP. That amount is destroyed and works as an anti-spam mechanism. The value in fiat depends on XRP price, but the operational cost remains extremely low for micropayments and high-volume activity.

Speed (3-5 seconds)

XRPL uses UNL-based consensus. Independent validators exchange proposals for the next ledger and, when roughly 80% agree, the ledger closes. There is no mining, which is why settlement happens in seconds.

Circulation volume and data sources

The main number represents the total XRP moved in 24h. Other data points may isolate specific flows such as issued assets or exchange-related transfers. Exact scope depends on the upstream API and should always be interpreted as an educational estimate first.

DEX-related transactions

This includes offers created or executed on the native DEX and payments that use automatic conversion through the order book. It reflects more complex financial activity than simple balance transfers.

Accounts

This proxy attempts to estimate how many XRPL accounts were active in the period. Since each account requires a base XRP reserve, this can hint at user, project, or application activity, but it is not a direct count from the protocol.
